# Research State

**Last updated:** 2026-10-06 (baseline-reference reconciliation; no new research or performance collection)

**Current objective:** determine what BG Cyber Deals should become on social media so Bowling Green residents voluntarily follow it and local merchants value participation.

## Business context

The current organic footprint is small and largely founder-invitation driven. Historic outsourced content was generic. Direct merchant outreach and an initial free period can supply both marketplace acquisition and featureable stories/assets, but should not turn the consumer feed into advertising.

## Leading hypotheses

- `H-001` Discovery-first local content can outperform standalone coupon messaging. **Moderate; researching.**
- `H-002` Human/owner/staff-led content can outperform static branded promotion. **Moderate; supported externally, untested internally.**
- `H-003` Merchant resharing can be an early distribution engine. **Low-to-moderate; proposed for testing.**
- `H-004` Facebook, Instagram, and LinkedIn need distinct jobs. **Moderate; supported by platform use patterns, untested internally.**

## Most useful current evidence

The 141-observation Bowling Green Facebook sample includes strong engagement around time-bounded family events, planning information, local food/seasonal rituals, sports/community identity, and personality-led local business content. It also shows weak visible response to generic newsletters, vague seasonal copy, routine closure notices, and generic inventory. A first analogue extension adds Everyday BG, Nashville Guru, Louisville Family Fun, and the BG Chamber's reshare/collaboration guide. See `research/bowling-green-market.md`, `research/competitors-and-analogues.md`, and the formal run.

## Material uncertainties

- The Facebook sample is not yet saturated (29 sources vs. a 40-source target) and is not proof of consumer conversion.
- The current audience is too invitation-driven to establish organic content-market fit.
- Instagram, LinkedIn, analogues in comparable cities, merchant resharing, and offer framing need structured evidence.
- A Facebook baseline was captured on 2026-09-10 for Aug 13–Sep 9 in `performance/experiment-results.md` and `performance/metrics.csv`. It does not attribute follows to invitation versus organic discovery. No controlled experiment results have been recorded.

## Priority backlog

1. Run a controlled merchant-feature/standalone-deal content experiment.
2. Establish clean baseline metrics and earned-follower tracking.
3. Research comparable local discovery brands and platform-specific execution.
4. Continue Facebook attention listening only where it fills source/segment gaps.
